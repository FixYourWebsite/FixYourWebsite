## `// ABOUT`

Your website is your storefront. When it's slow, broken or vulnerable, you lose customers and trust.

I'm a freelance web developer who **builds, repairs and hardens websites**: Drupal, custom PHP and JavaScript, responsive interfaces, plus security reviews based on the OWASP approach.

You get working code, plain-language communication, and reports you can actually act on. No jargon walls, no vague promises.

## `// HOW I WORK`

1. **Brief.** You tell me the goal or the problem. I ask only what I need.
2. **Plan.** I reply with scope, approach and a clear next step.
3. **Build or fix.** I work in Git, keep changes clean, and keep you updated.
